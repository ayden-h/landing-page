## Supplied solution 1

* each monochromatic triangle has a charge of +6,
* each bichromatic triangle has a charge of 0, and
* each trichromatic triangle has a charge of -3.
Since each vee contributes to exactly one triangle, we obtain that the total charge is $6A - 3B$.

\begin{align*}
& 2 \left[ \binom{a}{2} + \binom{b}{2} + \binom{c}{2} \right] - (ab + ac + bc) \\
&= (a^2 - a + b^2 - b + c^2 - c) - (ab + ac + bc) \\
&= (a^2 + b^2 + c^2 - ab - ac - bc) - (a + b + c) \\
&= (a^2 + b^2 + c^2 - ab - ac - bc) - (n - 1) \\
&\ge -(n - 1).
\end{align*}
$$

$$
6A - 3B \ge -n(n-1) \iff B \le 2A + \frac{n(n-1)}{3}
$$
