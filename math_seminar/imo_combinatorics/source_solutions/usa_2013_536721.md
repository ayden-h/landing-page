## Supplied solution 1

We will prove by induction that, for all $n \ge 2$,
$$
1^1 + 3^3 + 5^5 + \dots + (2^n - 1)^{(2^n - 1)} \equiv 2^n \pmod{2^{n+1}}. \quad (40)
$$
The base case $n = 2$ is straightforward. Assume that (40) holds for a given $n \ge 2$; we will prove it for $n + 1$; that is, to prove that
$$
1^1 + 3^3 + 5^5 + \dots + (2^{n+1} - 1)^{(2^{n+1} - 1)} \equiv 2^{n+1} \pmod{2^{n+2}}. \quad (41)
$$
For $x = 1, 3, \dots, 2^n - 1$, we pair the terms $x^x$ and $(x+2^n)^{(x+2^n)}$ on the left-hand side of (41). When $(x+2^n)^{(x+2^n)}$ is expanded using the binomial theorem, all terms except the first two can be ignored since they are divisible by $2^{2n}$. Therefore, we have, modulo $2^{2n}$, that
$$
x^x + (x + 2^n)^{(x+2^n)} \equiv x^x + x^x + (x + 2^n) \cdot 2^n \cdot x^{x-1} \equiv 2x^x + 2^n \cdot x^x \equiv x^x(2 + 2^n).
$$
Summing over all $x = 1, 3, \dots, 2^n - 1$, we get, modulo $2^{n+2}$, that
$$
\begin{aligned}
1^1 + 3^3 + \dots + (2^{n+1} - 1)^{(2^{n+1}-1)} &\equiv (1^1 + 3^3 + \dots + (2^n - 1)^{(2^n-1)}) (2 + 2^n) \\
&\equiv 2^n \cdot (2 + 2^n) \\
&\equiv 2^{n+1}.
\end{aligned}
$$
