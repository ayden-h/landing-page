## Supplied solution 1

Let $M$ be a subset of the set $\{1,2, \ldots, 1998\}$ and let $|M|=k$. Then the set $M$ can be obtained as the union of $t$ sets $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{t}$ in $(2^{t}-1)^{k}$ different ways since each element $x \in M$ can belong to $2^{t}-1$ nonempty families of subsets $A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{t}$.

Thus we have
$$
\sum_{(A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots, A_{t}) \in F} |A_{1} \cup A_{2} \cup \ldots \cup A_{t}| = \sum_{k=1}^{1998} k \binom{1998}{k} (2^{t}-1)^{k}
$$

$$
= 1998 (2^{t}-1) \sum_{k=0}^{1997} \binom{1997}{k} (2^{t}-1)^{k} = 1998 (2^{t}-1) 2^{1997 t}.
$$
