## Supplied solution 1

Suppose $|S| = s$. Then $\sum_{(a, b, c) \in S} a \ge \sum_{i=0}^{s-1} i = \frac{s(s-1)}{2}$. Likewise $\sum_S b \ge \frac{s(s-1)}{2}$ and $\sum_S c \ge \frac{s(s-1)}{2}$. Therefore
$$
\frac{3s(s-1)}{2} \le \sum_{S} a + b + c = ns \Rightarrow s \le \left\lfloor \frac{2n}{3} \right\rfloor + 1.
$$
