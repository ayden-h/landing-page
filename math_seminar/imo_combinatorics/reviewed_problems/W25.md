# W25. A private witness in every proper-union subfamily

**Source:** RMM 2017, Problem 3. **Record:** `rou_2017_f8413b`.

Let \(|X|=n>1\). A family of distinct proper nonempty subsets of \(X\) has the following property: every nonempty subfamily whose union is not \(X\) has an element belonging to exactly one member of that subfamily. Find its maximum size.
