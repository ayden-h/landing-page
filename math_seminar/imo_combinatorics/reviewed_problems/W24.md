# W24. The fewest tiles in a maximal placement

**Source:** EGMO 2016, Problem 5. **Record:** `rou_2016_9aa109`.

Let \(k\ge2\) and \(k\le n\le2k-1\). Place nonoverlapping \(1\times k\) or \(k\times1\) tiles on an \(n\times n\) board until no further tile fits. What is the smallest possible number of tiles?
