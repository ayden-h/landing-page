# W02. Two covering counts have the same parity

**Source:** RMM 2012, Problem 1. **Record:** `rmm_2012_b79110`.

In a finite bipartite acquaintance graph with parts \(L,R\), a subset of either part covers the other part when every vertex of the other part has a neighbor in it. Prove that the numbers of covering subsets on the two sides have the same parity.
