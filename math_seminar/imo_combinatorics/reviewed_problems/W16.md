# W16. Knights against blocking queens

**Source:** IMO Shortlist 2018, C2. **Record:** `imo_2018_eed763`.

Horst places a knight on an empty square of a \(20\times20\) board without attacking any previous knight. Queenie then occupies an empty square with a queen. They repeat until a player cannot move. Queens only occupy squares; they impose no attack restriction. What number of knights can Horst guarantee?
