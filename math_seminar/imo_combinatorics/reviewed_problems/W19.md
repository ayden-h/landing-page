# W19. A set whose largest half is constrained

**Source:** USAMO 2006, Problem 2. **Record:** `usa_2006_9454b3`.

For a positive integer \(k\), find the least \(N\) for which \(2k+1\) distinct positive integers have total sum greater than \(N\), while every \(k\)-element subset has sum at most \(N/2\).
