# W23. A finite state space with draws

**Source:** Balkan Mathematical Olympiad 2018, Problem 3. **Record:** `mkd_2018_3ac94a`.

Starting from positive piles \((a,b)\), Alice and Bob alternate, Alice first. A move chooses an even pile and transfers half of it to the other pile. A player with no legal move loses. Determine exactly when Bob can force a win.
