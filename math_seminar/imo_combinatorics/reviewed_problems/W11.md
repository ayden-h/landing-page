# W11. A sum-free sequence at the density boundary

**Source:** USA TSTST 2013, Problem 1, as identified in the recovered selection booklet. **Record:** `usa_2013_bfa49c`.

Find all strictly increasing positive integer sequences \((a_i)\) with no equation \(a_i+a_j=a_k\), allowing repeated indices, and with \(a_k=2k-1\) for infinitely many \(k\).
