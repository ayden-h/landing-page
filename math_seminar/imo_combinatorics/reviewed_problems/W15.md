# W15. The anti-Pascal pyramid

**Source:** IMO 2018, Problem 3; also IMO Shortlist 2018, C4. **Record:** `imo_2018_55614a`.

In a triangular array, every entry above the bottom row equals the absolute difference of its two children. Can an array with 2018 rows contain every integer from 1 through \(1+2+\cdots+2018\) exactly once?
