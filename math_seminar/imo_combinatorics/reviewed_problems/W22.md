# W22. Cookie pebbling on an even cycle

**Source:** Korean Mathematical Olympiad 2010, Problem 5. National pipeline supplement. **Record:** `kor_2010_1dc4be`.

At the vertices of a cycle with \(2n\) vertices are nonnegative integer numbers of cookies. A move consumes two cookies at a vertex and places one at a neighboring vertex. Find the smallest total number guaranteeing that a prescribed vertex can obtain a cookie, regardless of the initial distribution.
