# W21. Equal totals but strongly unbalanced rows and columns

**Source:** Vietnam Mathematical Olympiad 2016, Day 1, Problem 4. **Record:** `vnm_2016_c11c43`.

An \(m\times n\) board contains equal numbers of two symbols. In each row their counts differ by at least \(n/2\), and in each column by at least \(m/2\). Show that \(m,n\) are divisible by four, and construct such a board for \(m=n=2016\).
