# W17. One spanning tree meeting three quotas

**Source:** RMM 2023, Problem 6. **Record:** `rou_2023_0cea08`.

A connected graph has \(r+g+b+1\) vertices, with red, green and blue edges. There is a spanning tree with exactly \(r\) red edges, another with exactly \(g\) green edges, and another with exactly \(b\) blue edges. Prove that one tree meets all three quotas simultaneously.
