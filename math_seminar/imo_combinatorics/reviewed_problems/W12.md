# W12. Large disjoint cross-intersecting families

**Source:** IMO Shortlist 2007, C7. **Record:** `imo_2007_67267d`.

For every \(0<\alpha<(3-\sqrt5)/2\), find \(n,p\) with \(p>\alpha2^n\) and two disjoint families \(\mathcal S,\mathcal T\), each of \(p\) distinct subsets of \([n]\), such that each member of \(\mathcal S\) intersects every member of \(\mathcal T\).
