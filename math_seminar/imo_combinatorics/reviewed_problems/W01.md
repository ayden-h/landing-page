# W01. A gcd–lcm process

**Source:** IMO 2026, Problem 1; [official paper](https://www.imo-official.org/assets/documents/problems/2026/2026_eng.pdf). Mixed number theory and combinatorics.

Begin with 2026 integers greater than one. A move chooses two current entries \(a,b>1\) and replaces them by \(g=\gcd(a,b)\) and \(\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)/g\). Prove that play must end with exactly one entry greater than one, and that this final entry is independent of all choices.
