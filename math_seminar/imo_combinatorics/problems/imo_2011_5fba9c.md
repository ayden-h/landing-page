## Problem

Determine all sequences $\left(x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{2011}\right)$ of positive integers such that for every positive integer $n$ there is an integer $a$ with
$$
x_{1}^{n}+2 x_{2}^{n}+\cdots+2011 x_{2011}^{n}=a^{n+1}+1 .
$$
