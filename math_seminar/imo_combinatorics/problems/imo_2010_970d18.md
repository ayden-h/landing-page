## Problem

Let $\mathbb{N}$ be the set of all positive integers. Find all functions $f: \mathbb{N} \rightarrow \mathbb{N}$ such that the number $(f(m)+n)(m+f(n))$ is a square for all $m, n \in \mathbb{N}$.
(U.S.A.)
