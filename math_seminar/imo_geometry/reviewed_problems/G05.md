# G05. Reflected lines meet on the mirror

**Source:** IMO shortlist 2013, G2. **Record:** `imo_2013_22bd4a`.

Let $M,N$ be the midpoints of $AB,AC$, and $T$ the midpoint of the arc $BC$ not containing $A$ on $(ABC)$. The circle $(AMT)$ meets the perpendicular bisector of $AC$ at an interior point $X$ of $ABC$; similarly $(ANT)$ meets the perpendicular bisector of $AB$ at an interior point $Y$. If $K=MN\cap XY$, prove $KA=KT$.
