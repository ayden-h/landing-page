# G13. A circle equation linearizes the ratio

**Source:** IMO shortlist 2015, G4. **Record:** `imo_2015_b11e64`.

In an acute triangle $ABC$, let $M$ be the midpoint of $AC$. A circle through $B,M$ meets $BA,BC$ again at $P,Q$. Complete parallelogram $BPTQ$, and assume $T\in(ABC)$. Determine $BT/BM$.
