# G01. Two similarities sharing a secant

**Source:** IMO shortlist 2015, G1. **Record:** `imo_2015_29f541`.

In an acute triangle $ABC$ with orthocenter $H$, complete the parallelogram $ABGH$. On line $GH$ choose $I$ so that $AC$ passes through the midpoint of $HI$. Let $J\ne C$ be the other intersection of $AC$ with $(GCI)$. Prove $IJ=AH$.
