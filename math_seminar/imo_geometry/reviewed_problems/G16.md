# G16. Reflect a vertex in the incenter

**Source:** IMO shortlist 2024, G2. **Record:** `imo_2024_7db46b`.

A triangle has $AB<AC<BC$, incenter $I$, and incircle. The other tangent parallel to $AC$ meets the interior of $BC$ at $X$; the other tangent parallel to $AB$ meets it at $Y$. Let $P\ne A$ be the intersection of $AI$ with $(ABC)$, and let $K,L$ be the midpoints of $AB,AC$. Prove $\angle KIL+\angle YPX=180^\circ$.
