# G08. Convert intersection ratios into half angles

**Source:** IMO shortlist 2016, G2. **Record:** `imo_2016_022acb`.

Let $I$ be the incenter, $M$ the midpoint of $BC$, and $D$ the foot from $I$ to $BC$. The perpendicular to $AI$ through $I$ meets $AB,AC$ at $F,E$. Let $X\ne A$ be the other common point of $(AEF)$ and $(ABC)$. Prove that $XD$ and $AM$ meet on $(ABC)$.
