# G12. Close two circles around the target point

**Source:** IMO shortlist 2021, G1. **Record:** `imo_2021_18d688`.

In parallelogram $ABCD$, assume $AC=BC$. Take $P$ beyond $B$ on $AB$. Let $Q\ne D$ be the intersection of $PD$ with $(ACD)$, and $R\ne P$ the intersection of $PC$ with $(APQ)$. Prove $CD,AQ,BR$ concurrent.
