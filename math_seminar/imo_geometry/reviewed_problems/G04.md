# G04. Reflection completes a cyclicity proof

**Source:** IMO shortlist 2012, G2. **Record:** `imo_2012_758467`.

In cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$, the diagonals meet at $E$. The extensions of $AD,BC$ beyond $A,B$ meet at $F$. Complete parallelogram $ECGD$, and reflect $E$ in $AD$ to obtain $H$. Prove $D,H,F,G$ concyclic.
