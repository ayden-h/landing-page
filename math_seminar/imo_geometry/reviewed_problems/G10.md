# G10. A half-turn creates the tangent

**Source:** IMO 2017, Problem 4; shortlist G2. **Record:** `imo_2017_bb6ddb`.

Points $R,S$ lie on $\Omega$, and $R'=2S-R$. Let $I$ lie on the smaller arc $RS$. The circle $\Gamma=(ISR')$ meets the tangent to $\Omega$ at $R$ twice; call the nearer intersection to $R$ point $A$. If $AI$ meets $\Omega$ again at $J$, prove $JR'$ tangent to $\Gamma$.
