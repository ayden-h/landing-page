# G14. Transfer a tangent through equal powers

**Source:** IMO shortlist 2015, G6. **Record:** `imo_2015_d499b1`.

An acute triangle $ABC$ has $AB>AC$, orthocenter $H$, base midpoint $M$, and altitude foot $F$ on $BC$. On its circumcircle $\Omega$, choose $Q,K$ so that $\angle AQH=\angle QKH=90^\circ$. Prove that $(KQH)$ and $(KFM)$ are tangent at $K$.
