# G15. Equal segments turn spiral similarity into rotation

**Source:** IMO shortlist 2024, G1. **Record:** `imo_2024_a60b6c`.

A cyclic quadrilateral satisfies $AC<BD<AD$ and $\angle DBA<90^\circ$. On the side of $AD$ opposite $C$, choose $E$ with $DE\parallel AB$, $DE=AC$, and $F$ with $AF\parallel CD$, $AF=BD$. Prove that the perpendicular bisectors of $BC,EF$ meet on $(ABCD)$.
