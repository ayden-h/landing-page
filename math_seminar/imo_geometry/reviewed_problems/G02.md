# G02. Reflect the two lines

**Source:** IMO shortlist 2015, G2. **Record:** `imo_2015_7c01e3`.

Triangle $ABC$ lies on a circle $\Omega$ with center $O$. A circle centered at $A$ cuts $BC$ at $D,E$ in the order $B,D,E,C$, and cuts the arcs $AB$ and $AC$ of $\Omega$ not containing the third vertex at $F,G$, respectively. Let $K\ne B$ be the intersection of $AB$ with $(BDF)$, and $L\ne C$ the intersection of $AC$ with $(CEG)$. If distinct lines $FK,GL$ meet at $X$, prove $A,X,O$ collinear.
