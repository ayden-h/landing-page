## Supplied solution 1

Let $x$ be the length of a side of the square $ABCD$. Then, we have $ZS = x - 7$ and $DS = x - 6$. Applying the Pythagorean theorem to the right triangle $ZSD$, we get $(x - 7)^2 + (x - 6)^2 = 5^2$, from which we get $x = 3, 10$. But from $x > BP$ we conclude that $x = 10$, and it is easy to check that $x = 10$ does satisfy the requirement of the problem.
