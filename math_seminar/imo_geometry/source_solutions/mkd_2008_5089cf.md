## Supplied solution 1

We have that $\alpha + \beta = \frac{5}{6} \cdot 90^\circ = 75^\circ$ and $\alpha - \beta = 20^\circ$.

Then $\beta = (75^\circ - 20^\circ) : 2 = 27^\circ 30'$ and $\alpha = 75^\circ - 27^\circ 30' = 47^\circ 30'$, so we have that $\gamma = 180^\circ - (\alpha + \beta) = 180^\circ - 75^\circ = 105^\circ$.

The triangle is obtuse-angled.
