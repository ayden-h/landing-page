## Supplied solution 1

Let the size of the two acute angles be $x$ (they are equal). Then each of the obtuse angles equals $180^{\circ} - x$. From the condition in the problem we have that $2x = \frac{1}{2}(180^{\circ} - x)$, i.e. $4x = 180^{\circ} - x$, from where we obtain $x = 36^{\circ}$. Hence each of the obtuse angles equals $180^{\circ} - 36^{\circ} = 144^{\circ}$.
