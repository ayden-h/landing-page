## Supplied solution 1

The given inequality is equivalent to
$$
\frac{m_a}{a^2}(a-b)(a-c) + \frac{m_b}{b^2}(b-a)(b-c) + \frac{m_c}{c^2}(c-a)(c-b) \geq 0.
$$
The last one is symmetric with respect to the variables, so without loss of generality, we can assume that $a \geq b \geq c$. Then we have that
$$
\frac{m_a}{a^2}(a-b)(a-c) \geq 0,
$$
so it is enough to prove that
$$
\frac{m_b}{b^2}(b-a)(b-c) + \frac{m_c}{c^2}(c-a)(c-b) \geq 0. \qquad (9)
$$
Using the fact that $a - c \geq a - b$ we can write
$$
\begin{aligned} 
\frac{m_b}{b^2}(b-a)(b-c) + \frac{m_c}{c^2}(c-a)(c-b) &= (b-c)\left((a-c)\frac{m_c}{c^2} - (a-b)\frac{m_b}{b^2}\right) \\ 
&\geq (b-c)\left((a-b)\frac{m_c}{c^2} - (a-b)\frac{m_b}{b^2}\right) \\ 
&= (a-b)(b-c)\left(\frac{m_c}{c^2} - \frac{m_b}{b^2}\right) 
\end{aligned} \qquad (10)
$$
We finally prove that
$$
\frac{m_c}{m_b} \geq \frac{c}{b} \geq \frac{c^2}{b^2}. \qquad (11)
$$
Indeed, the last inequality holds since $\frac{c}{b} \geq \frac{c^2}{b^2}$ and
$$
\begin{aligned} 
\frac{m_c}{m_b} \geq \frac{c}{b} &\iff \frac{\sqrt{2a^2 + 2b^2 - c^2}}{\sqrt{2a^2 + 2c^2 - b^2}} \geq \frac{c}{b} \\ 
&\iff b^2(2a^2 + 2b^2 - c^2) \geq c^2(2a^2 + 2c^2 - b^2) \\ 
&\iff b^2(2a^2 + 2b^2) \geq c^2(2a^2 + 2c^2), 
\end{aligned}
$$
which holds since $b^2 \geq c^2$ and $2a^2 + 2b^2 \geq 2a^2 + 2c^2$.
Combining (10) and (11) we obtain the desired inequality (9).
