## Supplied solution 1

Since $AB = 3$, $BC = 4$ and $\angle ABC = 90^\circ$, we get $AC = 5$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $AD$. Because $AM = DM$, $MC = MC$, $AC = 5 = DC$ and $\angle ABC = \angle AMC = 90^\circ$, $AB = AM$, $AC = AC$, the triangles $ABC$, $AMC$ and $DMC$ are all congruent.

Therefore, the area of $ABCD$ is $\frac{3 \times 4}{2} \times 3 = 18$.
