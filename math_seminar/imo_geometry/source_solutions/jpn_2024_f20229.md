## Supplied solution 1

$5 + \sqrt{10}$

Let $x = BX$. Since $CX = 10 - x$ and $BX > CX$, we have $5 < x < 10$. By the power of a point theorem,
$$
x^2 + (10-x)^2 = BX^2 + CX^2 = BD \cdot BA + CE \cdot CA = 10(BD + CE)
$$
holds. Also, from the condition $AD + AE = 13$, we find
$$
BD + CE = (10 - AD) + (10 - AE) = 20 - (AD + AE) = 7
$$
and thus
$$
x^2 + (10 - x)^2 = 70
$$
Since $5 < x < 10$, this yields $x = 5 + \sqrt{10}$.
