## Supplied solution 2

We work with directed angles. Let $H K$ meet $B C$ at $X$. Let $M X$ meet $A H$ at $H_{A}$ on $\Gamma$ (where $H_{A}$ is the reflection of $H$ in $B C$). Define $E'$ to be where $H_{A} M$ meets $\Gamma$ (again). Our task is to show that $\angle M E' B = \angle M L B$.
Observe that
$$
\begin{aligned}
\angle M E' B & = \angle H_{A} A B & \text{ (angles in same segment) } \\
& = B^{c} &
\end{aligned}
$$
Now
$$
\begin{aligned}
\angle M L B & = \angle H L B \\
& = \angle B K H_{C} \\
& = \angle B C H_{C} \\
& = B^{c} .
\end{aligned}
$$
