## Supplied solution 1

For the angles $\alpha$ and $\beta$ we have $\alpha + \beta = 90^\circ$ and $\alpha - \beta = 20^\circ 52'$. Hence $\beta = (90^\circ - 20^\circ 52') : 2 = 34^\circ 34'$ and $\alpha = 90^\circ - 34^\circ 34' = 55^\circ 26'.$
