## Supplied solution 1

Since $AB \parallel DC$ and since $\angle DCA$ and $\angle DBA$ are angles subtended by the same arc $\widearc{AD}$ at the points $C$ and $B$ on the circle, we have $\angle BDC = \angle DBA = \angle DCA$.

Since the angle subtended by an arc $\widearc{AB}$ at the point $C$ on the circle equals the angle formed by the tangent line to the circle at $B$ and the chord $AB$, which is $30^\circ$, we have $\angle ACB = 30^\circ$.

Similarly, we have $\angle DBC = 10^\circ$.

Therefore, the sum of the inner angles of the triangle $BCD$ equals
$$
\angle BDC + \angle DCA + \angle ACB + \angle DBC = 2\angle BDC + 30^\circ + 10^\circ,
$$
from which we get
$$
\angle BDC = \frac{180^\circ - (10^\circ + 30^\circ)}{2} = 70^\circ.
$$
