## Supplied solution 1

Denote by $A$, $B$ and $C$ the three apexes of the triangle and by $a$ the length of one edge. Then
$$
\triangle ABC = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} a^2, \text{ and}
$$
$$
\triangle ABC = \triangle ABP + \triangle BCP + \triangle CAP = \frac{1}{2} \cdot (1 + 2 + 3) \cdot a = 3a.
$$

Therefore $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}a^2 = 3a$ and hence the answer is $a = 4\sqrt{3}$.
