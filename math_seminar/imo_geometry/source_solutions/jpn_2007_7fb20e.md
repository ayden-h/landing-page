## Supplied solution 1

Take $\angle APB = \theta$ and let $S$ be the area of $APB$. Then $\frac{1}{2} \times AP \times BP \times \sin \theta = S = \frac{3 \times 7}{2} = \frac{21}{2}$. Since $\sin \theta$ is positive, $AP \times BP$ takes minimum value when $\sin \theta$ takes maximum. Since $\frac{7}{2} > 3$, we can take $P$ on the circle with diameter $AB$. Then $\sin \theta$ takes maximum value $1$ and $AP \times BP$ takes minimum value $21$.
