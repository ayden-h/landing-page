## Supplied solution 1

Let $a$, $b$, $c$ be the sides of a triangle. From the triangle inequality $a + b > c$, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
a^3 + b^3 + 3abc &= (a+b)(a^2 - ab + b^2) + 3abc \\&> c \cdot (a^2 - ab + b^2) + 3abc \\
&= c \cdot (a^2 - ab + b^2 + 3ab) \\
&= c \cdot (a^2 + 2ab + b^2) \\
&= c \cdot (a+b)^2 \\
&> c \cdot c^2 = c^3
\end{aligned}
$$
