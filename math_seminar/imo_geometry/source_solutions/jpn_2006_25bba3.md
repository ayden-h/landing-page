## Supplied solution 1

Since $\angle ACM = \angle BDM$ (because of $MA = MB$), $\angle PCQ = \angle PDQ$, so four points $C$, $D$, $P$ and $Q$ are concyclic. So $\angle PQD = \angle PCD = \angle ACD = \angle ABD$. Therefore $AB$ and $PQ$, hence $AB$ and $XY$ are parallel. Since $M$ is the middle point of the arc $AB$, it is also that of the arc $XY$. Thus $MX = MY$.
