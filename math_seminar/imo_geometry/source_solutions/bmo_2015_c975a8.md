## Supplied solution 1

The following are well known identities relating $r$, $R$, $s$:
$$
a+b+c=2s, \quad ab+bc+ca=s^2+r^2+4Rr, \quad abc=4Rrs
$$
Then
$$
\frac{1}{a+b} + \frac{1}{a+c} + \frac{1}{b+c} \le \frac{r}{16Rs} + \frac{s}{16Rr} + \frac{11}{8s}
$$
$$
\Leftrightarrow 4 \left( \frac{1}{a+b} + \frac{1}{a+c} + \frac{1}{b+c} \right) \le \frac{s^2+r^2+4rR}{4Rrs} + \frac{9}{2s}
$$
$$
4 \left( \frac{1}{a+b} + \frac{1}{b+c} + \frac{1}{a+c} \right) \le \frac{ab+ac+bc}{abc} + \frac{9}{a+b+c}
$$
$$
\Leftrightarrow 4 \left( \frac{1}{a+b} + \frac{1}{b+c} + \frac{1}{a+c} \right) \le \frac{9}{a+b+c} + \frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{c}.
$$
The last expression is Popoviciu's inequality for the function $f(x) = \frac{1}{x}$.
