## Supplied solution 1

We have
$$
\frac{\overline{AH}}{HA_1} + 1 = \frac{\overline{AH}}{HA_1} + \frac{\overline{HA_1}}{HA_1} = \frac{\overline{AA_1}}{HA_1} = \frac{P_{\triangle ABC}}{P_{\triangle HBC}}.
$$
Similarly, $\frac{\overline{BH}}{HB_1} + 1 = \frac{P_{\triangle HBC}}{P_{\triangle HCA}}$ and $\frac{\overline{CH}}{HC_1} + 1 = \frac{P_{\triangle HBC}}{P_{\triangle HAB}}$. If $x = P_{\triangle HBC}$, $y = P_{\triangle HCA}$, $z = P_{\triangle HAB}$, then

$P_{\triangle ABC} = x + y + z$. Now $\frac{\overline{AH}}{HA_1} + \frac{\overline{BH}}{HB_1} + \frac{\overline{CH}}{HC_1} = 2008$ is equivalent to
$$
\frac{x+y+z}{x} - 1 + \frac{x+y+z}{y} - 1 + \frac{x+y+z}{z} - 1 = 2008,
$$
from where $(x+y+z)(yz+xz+xy) = 2011xyz$.
Finally
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \left(\frac{x+y+z}{x}-1\right) \cdot \left(\frac{x+y+z}{y}-1\right) \cdot \left(\frac{x+y+z}{z}-1\right) = \frac{y+z}{x} \cdot \frac{x+z}{y} \cdot \frac{x+y}{z} = \frac{(y+z)(x+z)(x+y)}{xyz} = \\
& = \frac{(x+y+z)(yz+xz+xy)-xyz}{xyz} = \frac{2011xyz-xyz}{xyz} = 2010
\end{aligned}
$$
