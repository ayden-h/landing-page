## Supplied solution 2

Lines $YQ$ and $YR$ are perpendicular to $BX$ and $AZ$, respectively, so $\angle RYQ$ is equal to the acute angle between lines $BX$ and $AZ$. This angle is $\frac{1}{2}(\widehat{AX} + \widehat{BZ}) = \frac{1}{2}(180^\circ - \widehat{XZ})$ because $X$, $Z$ lie on the circle with diameter $AB$. Also, $\angle AXB = \angle AZB = 90^\circ$ and so $PXQY$ and $SZRY$ are rectangles, whence $\angle PQY = 90^\circ - \angle YXB = 90^\circ - \widehat{YB}/2$ and $\angle YRS = 90^\circ - \angle AZY = 90^\circ - \widehat{AY}/2$. The angle between $PQ$ and $RS$ is therefore
$$
\angle PQY + \angle YRS - \angle RYQ = (90^\circ - \frac{\widehat{YB}}{2}) + (90^\circ - \frac{\widehat{AY}}{2}) - (90^\circ - \frac{\widehat{XZ}}{2}) = \frac{\widehat{XZ}}{2} = \frac{\angle XOZ}{2},
$$
as desired.
