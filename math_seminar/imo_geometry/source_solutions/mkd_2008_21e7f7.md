## Supplied solution 1

The four arcs form a circumference with radius $r = 2\,\mathrm{cm}$. We have that $L = 2r\pi = 4\pi\,\mathrm{cm}$ and
$$
P = 4^2 - r^2\pi = 16 - 4\pi = 4(4 - \pi)\,\mathrm{cm}^2
$$
