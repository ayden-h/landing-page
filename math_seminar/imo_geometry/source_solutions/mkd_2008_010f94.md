## Supplied solution 1

$2 \cdot L_{\Delta ADC} = L_{\Delta ABC} + 2 \cdot \overline{CD}$. From here, we obtain $2 \cdot 29 = 36 + 2 \cdot \overline{CD}$ or $\overline{CD} = (2 \cdot 29 - 36) : 2 = 11\,\text{cm}$. Let $M$ be the middle point of $AD$ and $N$ be the middle point of $AC$. Then $MN$ is the middle line of the triangle $ADC$ so we have $\overline{MN} = \frac{1}{2} \overline{CD} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 11 = 5.5\,\text{cm}$.
