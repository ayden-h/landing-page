## Supplied solution 1

Take points $P$, $Q$, $R$ on the line segments $OA$, $OB$, $OC$, respectively, in such a way that the conditions $OP = 1$, $\angle OPQ = \angle OPR = 90^\circ$ are satisfied. Since $\angle POQ = 45^\circ$, we have $PQ = 1$ and $OQ = \sqrt{2}$. Similarly, we get $PR = 1$ and $OR = \sqrt{2}$. From $\angle ROQ = 60^\circ$ and $OR = OQ$, we see that the triangle $\triangle OQR$ is an equilateral triangle, so that we have that $QR = \sqrt{2}$. We then have $QR^2 = PQ^2 + PR^2$, from which we conclude that $\angle RPO = 90^\circ$. This means that the planes $AOB$ and $AOC$ intersect perpendicularly. Consequently, the foot $H$ of the perpendicular line segment drawn from the vertex $C$ to the plane $AOB$ must lie on the line $OA$, and we obtain $CH = OC \cdot \sin \angle AOC = \frac{5}{\sqrt{2}}$.

On the other hand, the area of the triangle $\triangle AOB$ equals
$$
\frac{1}{2} \cdot OA \cdot OB \cdot \sin \angle AOB = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 3 \cdot 4 \cdot \sin 45^\circ = 3\sqrt{2}
$$
and therefore, we conclude that the volume of the tetrahedron $OABC$ equals
$$
\frac{1}{3} \cdot 3\sqrt{2} \cdot \frac{5}{\sqrt{2}} = 5
$$
