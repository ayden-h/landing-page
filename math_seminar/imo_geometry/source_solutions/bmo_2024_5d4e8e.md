## Supplied solution 1

Since $AH \parallel DE$ we have:
$$
\angle DEX = \angle DEX = \angle DEX = \angle DEX
$$
So $DXEH$ is cyclic - call this circle $\omega$.
It's well-known that $AH = 2OM = ON$ so $NH = OA$ (which we'll use later) and $ON = AH = DO$ (as $ADOH$ is a parallelogram). This means $\frac{OD}{ND} = \frac{1}{2}$. Also, by considering homothety factor 2 at $O$:
$$
NX = 2MY = 2OA = 2OX \implies \frac{OX}{NX} = \frac{1}{2}
$$

$$
\frac{1}{2} = \frac{OH}{NH} = \frac{OH}{OA} \Rightarrow OA = 2OH
$$
which is what we wanted to prove. □
