## Supplied solution 2

Applying the properties of cross (double) ratio we get:
$$
(A, C; P, D) = Z(X, C; T, D) \stackrel{\omega}{\cong} Y(X, C; T, D) = (M, C; B, D)
$$
It follows that
$$
\frac{AP}{CP} : \frac{AD}{CD} = \frac{MB}{BC} : \frac{MD}{CD} \Rightarrow \frac{AP}{CP} = \frac{MB}{BC} \cdot \frac{AD}{MD} = \text{const}
$$
Therefore $P$ is a fixed point. $\square$
