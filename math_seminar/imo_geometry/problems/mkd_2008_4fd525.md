## Problem

Calculate the angles in the triangle $ABC$, if the angle between the altitude from $C$ and the bisector of the angle $ACB$ is $90^\circ$, and the angle between the bisectors of the exterior angles at the vertices $A$ and $B$ is $61^\circ$.
