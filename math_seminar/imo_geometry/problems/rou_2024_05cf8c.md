## Problem

Let $BC$ be a fixed segment in the plane, and let $A$ be a variable point in the plane not on the line $BC$. Distinct points $X$ and $Y$ are chosen on the rays $\vec{CA}$ and $\vec{BA}$, respectively, such that $\angle CBX = \angle YCB = \angle BAC$. Assume that the tangents to the circumcircle of $ABC$ at $B$ and $C$ meet line $XY$ at $P$ and $Q$, respectively, such that the points $X, P, Y$, and $Q$ are pairwise distinct and lie on the same side of $BC$. Let $\Omega_1$ be the circle through $X$ and $P$ centred on $BC$. Similarly, let $\Omega_2$ be the circle through $Y$ and $Q$ centred on $BC$. Prove that $\Omega_1$ and $\Omega_2$ intersect at two fixed points as $A$ varies.
