## Problem

Let $\triangle ABC$ be an acute triangle. Variable points $E$ and $F$ are on sides $AC$ and $AB$ respectively such that $BC^2 = BA \cdot BF + CE \cdot CA$. As $E$ and $F$ vary prove that the circumcircle of $AEF$ passes through a fixed point other than $A$.
