## Problem

Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle with circumcircle $\omega$. Let $t$ be a tangent line to $\omega$. Let $t_{a}, t_{b}$, and $t_{c}$ be the lines obtained by reflecting $t$ in the lines $BC, CA$, and $AB$, respectively. Show that the circumcircle of the triangle determined by the lines $t_{a}, t_{b}$, and $t_{c}$ is tangent to the circle $\omega$.
