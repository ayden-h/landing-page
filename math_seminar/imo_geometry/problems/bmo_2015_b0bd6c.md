## Problem

A quadrilateral $ABCD$ is given with $AD \parallel BC$. The midpoints of $AD$ and $BC$ are denoted by $M$ and $N$, respectively. The line $MN$ intersects the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ in points $K$ and $L$, respectively. Prove that the circumcircles of the triangles $AKM$ and $BNL$ have a common point on the line $AB$.
