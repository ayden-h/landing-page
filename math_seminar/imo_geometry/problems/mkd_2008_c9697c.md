## Problem

Prove that $\frac{\operatorname{tg}\alpha}{\operatorname{tg}\beta} = \frac{a^2+c^2-b^2}{b^2+c^2-a^2}$ for every triangle $ABC$ ($a, b, c$ and $\alpha, \beta$ are the sides and the angles in the triangle, respectively).
