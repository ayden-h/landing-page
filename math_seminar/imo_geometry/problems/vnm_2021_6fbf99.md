## Problem

Given a fixed circle ($O$) and two fixed points $B, C$ on that circle, let $A$ be a moving point on ($O$) such that triangle $ABC$ is acute and scalene. Let $I$ be the midpoint of $BC$ and let $AD, BE$ and $CF$ be the altitudes of triangle $ABC$. On two rays $\overrightarrow{FA}$, $\overrightarrow{EA}$, take $M, N$ such that $FM = CE$, $EN = BF$. Let $L$ be the intersection of $MN$ and $EF$, and let $G \neq L$ be the second intersection of the circumcircles of triangles $LEN$ and $LFM$.

a) Show that the circumcircle of triangle $MNG$ always goes through a fixed point.

b) Let $AD$ intersects ($O$) at $K$ which is differs from $A$. On the tangent line through $D$ of circumcircle of $DKI$, take $P, Q$ such that $GP$ is parallel to $AB$ and $GQ$ is parallel to $AC$. Let $T$ be the circumcenter of triangle $GPQ$. Show that $GT$ always goes through a fixed point.
