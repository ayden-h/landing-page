## Problem

Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle with fixed points $B, C$ and point $A$ moves on the big arc $BC$ of $(ABC)$ such that $AB \neq AC$. Incircle $(I)$ of triangle $ABC$ touches $BC$ at $D$. Let $I_a$ be the $A$-excenter of triangle $ABC$. $I_aD$ cuts $OI$ at $L$ and $E$ lies on $(I)$ such that $DE$ is parallel to $AI$.

a) $LE$ cuts $AI$ at $F$. Prove that $AF = AI$.

b) Let $M$ be the point the circumcircle $(J)$ of triangle $I_aBC$ such that $I_aM$ is parallel to $AI$. The line $MD$ cuts $(J)$ again at $N$. Prove that the midpoint $T$ of $MN$ lies on a fixed circle.
