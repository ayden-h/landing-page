## Problem

Let $B$ be an interior point of the segment $AC$. The equilateral triangles $\triangle ABM$ and $\triangle BCN$ are constructed in same half plane determined by the line $AC$. The lines $AN$ and $CM$ intersect in $L$. Find the angle $\angle CLN$.
