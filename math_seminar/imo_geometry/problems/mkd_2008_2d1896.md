## Problem

Let $k$ is the circumscribed circle for the quadrangle $ABCD$. The angle at the vertex $B$ is twice bigger than the angle at the vertex $A$ and for $40^\circ$ smaller than the angle at the vertex $D$. Calculate the angles of $ABCD$.
