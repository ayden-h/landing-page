## Problem

Let $ABC$ be an acute, scalene triangle with orthocenter $H$ and $D$, $E$, $F$ be the feet of altitudes from vertices $A$, $B$, $C$ respectively. Let $(I)$ be the circumcircle of triangle $HEF$ with centre $I$ and $K$, $J$ be the midpoints of $BC$, $EF$ respectively. $HJ$ meets $(I)$ again at $G$, $GK$ meets $(I)$ again at $L$.

a) Prove that $AL$ is perpendicular to $EF$.

b) Let $AL$ meet $EF$ at $M$, $IM$ meet the circumcircle of triangle $IEF$ again at $N$ and $DN$ meet $AB$, $AC$ at $P$, $Q$ respectively. Prove that $PE$, $QF$ and $AK$ are concurrent.
