## Problem

Let $\triangle ABC$ be a triangle with two fixed vertices $B, C$ and the vertex $A$ is variable. Let $H$ and $G$ be the orthocenter and the centroid of the triangle $ABC$ respectively. Find the locus of $A$ such that the midpoint $K$ of the segment $HG$ moves on the line $BC$.
