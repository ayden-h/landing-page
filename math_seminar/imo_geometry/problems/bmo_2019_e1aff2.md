## Problem

Let $ABCD$ be a square of center $O$ and let $M$ be the symmetric of the point $B$ with respect to the point $A$. Let $E$ be the intersection of $CM$ and $BD$, and let $S$ be the intersection of $MO$ and $AE$. Show that $SO$ is the angle bisector of $\angle ESB$.
