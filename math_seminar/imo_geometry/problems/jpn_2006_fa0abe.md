## Problem

The triangle $ABC$ satisfies $BC = 5$, $CA = 7$ and $AB = 8$. Let $O$ be a point inside the triangle $ABC$ such that the triangles $OBC$, $OCA$ and $OAB$ have the same circumradius. Find their common circumradius.
