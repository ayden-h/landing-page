## Problem

Let $A_1B_1C_1$ and $A_2B_2C_2$ are given triangles. Let $T_1$ and $T_2$ are their centers of mass correspondently. Prove that $3\overline{T_1T_2} = \overline{A_1A_2} + \overline{B_1B_2} + \overline{C_1C_2}$.
