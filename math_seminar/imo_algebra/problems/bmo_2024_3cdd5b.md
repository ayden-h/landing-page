## Problem

Find all triples $(a, b, c)$ of positive real numbers that satisfy the system:
$$
\begin{aligned}
11bc - 36b - 15c &= abc \\
12ca - 10c - 28a &= abc \\
13ab - 21a - 6b &= abc.
\end{aligned}
$$
