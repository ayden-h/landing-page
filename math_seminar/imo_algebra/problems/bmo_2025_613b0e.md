## Problem

Find all polynomials $P(x)$ with integer coefficients such that there exists a positive integer $N$ such that for all positive integers $n > N$ we have $P(n) > 0$ and
$$
n + P(n) \mid n^{P(n)} + P(n)^n.
$$
