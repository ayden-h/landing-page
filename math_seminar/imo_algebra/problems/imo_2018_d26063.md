## Problem

Determine all functions $f:(0, \infty) \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ satisfying
$$
\left(x+\frac{1}{x}\right) f(y)=f(x y)+f\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)
$$
for all $x, y>0$.
