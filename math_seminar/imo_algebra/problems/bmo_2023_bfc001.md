## Problem

Are there polynomials $P$ and $Q$ with real coefficients such that $P(P(x)) \cdot Q(Q(x))$ has exactly $2023$ distinct real roots and $P(Q(x)) \cdot Q(P(x))$ has exactly $2024$ distinct real roots?
