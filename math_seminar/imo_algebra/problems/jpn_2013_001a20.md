## Problem

Let for $k$, $0 \le k \le 9$, $a_k$ be the coefficient of the $x^k$-term in the polynomial $(x+1)^3(x+2)^3(x+3)^3$. Find the value of $a_2 + a_4 + a_6 + a_8$.
