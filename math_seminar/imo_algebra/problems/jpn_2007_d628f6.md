## Problem

Find all positive integer pairs $(m, n)$ that satisfy the following conditions.
$$
(1)\ m, n \leq 20
$$
(2) $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime.
$$
(3) \quad \frac{5}{7} < \frac{m}{n} < \frac{3}{4}
$$
