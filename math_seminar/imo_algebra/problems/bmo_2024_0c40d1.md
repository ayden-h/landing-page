## Problem

Let $k$ be a positive integer. Find all sequences $(a_n)_{n \ge 1}$ of positive integers such that
$$
a_{n+2}(a_{n+1} - k) = a_n(a_{n+1} + k)
$$
for all $n \ge 1$.
