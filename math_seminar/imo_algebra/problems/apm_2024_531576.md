## Problem

Let $n$ be a positive integer and $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ be positive real numbers. Prove that
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{1}{2^{i}}\left(\frac{2}{1+a_{i}}\right)^{2^{i}} \geq \frac{2}{1+a_{1} a_{2} \ldots a_{n}}-\frac{1}{2^{n}} .
$$
