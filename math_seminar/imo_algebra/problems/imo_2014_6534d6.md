## Problem

Let $z_{0} < z_{1} < z_{2} < \cdots$ be an infinite sequence of positive integers. Prove that there exists a unique integer $n \geqslant 1$ such that
$$
z_{n} < \frac{z_{0} + z_{1} + \cdots + z_{n}}{n} \leqslant z_{n+1} .
$$
