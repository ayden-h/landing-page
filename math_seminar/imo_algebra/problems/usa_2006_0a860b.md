## Problem

Find all positive integers $n$ such that there are $k \ge 2$ positive rational numbers $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_k$ satisfying $a_1 + a_2 + \dots + a_k = a_1 \cdot a_2 \dots a_k = n$.
