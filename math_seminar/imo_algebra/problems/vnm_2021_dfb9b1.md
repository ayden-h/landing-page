## Problem

Let $a$, $b$ and $c$ be non-negative real numbers such that
$$
2(a^2 + b^2 + c^2) + 3(ab + bc + ca) = 5(a + b + c).
$$
Prove that $4(a^2 + b^2 + c^2) + 2(ab + bc + ca) + 7abc \le 25$.
