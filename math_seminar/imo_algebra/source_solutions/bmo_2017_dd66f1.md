## Supplied solution 1

First we remark that
$$
a^5 + b^5 \ge ab(a^3 + b^3).
$$
Indeed
$$
\begin{aligned}
a^5 + b^5 \ge ab(a^3 + b^3) &\Leftrightarrow a^5 - a^4b - ab^4 + b^5 \ge 0 \\
&\Leftrightarrow (a-b)(a^4 - b^4) \ge 0 \\
&\Leftrightarrow (a-b)^2(a^2 + b^2)(a+b) \ge 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
We rewrite the inequality as
$$
\frac{1}{a^5+b^5+abc^3} + \frac{1}{b^5+c^5+bca^3} + \frac{1}{c^5+a^5+cab^3} \le 1
$$
On the other hand the following inequality is true
$$
a^5 + b^5 + abc^3 \ge ab(a^3 + b^3 + c^3),
$$
and similar for the other two.
Finally, using AM-GM we get:
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \frac{1}{a^5+b^5+c^2} + \frac{1}{b^5+c^5+a^2} + \frac{1}{c^5+a^5+b^2} \\
& \le \frac{1}{a^3+b^3+c^3} \left( \frac{1}{ab} + \frac{1}{bc} + \frac{1}{ca} \right) = \frac{a+b+c}{a^3+b^3+c^3} \\
& \le \frac{a+b+c}{(a+b+c)^3} = \frac{9}{(a+b+c)^2} \le \frac{9}{(3\sqrt[3]{abc})^2} = 1.
\end{aligned}
$$
