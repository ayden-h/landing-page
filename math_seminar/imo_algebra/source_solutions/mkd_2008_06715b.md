## Supplied solution 1

The given system is equivalent to
$$
\begin{cases}
xy + xz = 35 \\
yz + yx = 32 \\
zx + zy = 27
\end{cases}
$$
If $a = xy$, $b = xz$, $c = yz$ then the system is
$$
\begin{cases}
a+b=35 \quad ...(1) \\
c+a=32 \quad ...(2) \\
b+c=27 \quad ...(3)
\end{cases}
$$
Subtraction of the equations we have
(1) + (2) - (3): $2a = 40$, (2) + (3) - (1): $2c = 12$ and (1) + (3) - (2): $2b = 30$. From here we get
$$
\begin{cases}
a=20 \\
b=15 \\
c=12
\end{cases}
$$
i.e. $\begin{cases} xy=20 \\ xz=15 \\ yz=12 \end{cases}$, for $x,y,z \neq 0$. Now the system is $\begin{cases} y=\frac{20}{x} \\ z=\frac{15}{x} \\ yz=12 \end{cases}$.

Putting the first and the second equation in the third one we have
$$
\left(\frac{20}{x}\right)\left(\frac{15}{x}\right) = 12 \implies \frac{300}{x^2} = 12 \implies x^2 = 25,\ x = \pm 5.
$$
Then the solution of the system is $(x,y,z) \in \{(5,4,3),\ (-5,-4,-3)\}$.
