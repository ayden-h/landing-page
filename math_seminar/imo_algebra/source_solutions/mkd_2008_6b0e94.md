## Supplied solution 1

Let Stefan took with him $x$ denars, Filip $y$ and Nikola $z$. We have that $x + y + z = 222$.

On the end of the excursion Stefan has left $\frac{2}{3}x$, Filip $\frac{4}{5}y$ and Nikola $\frac{8}{15}z$ denars.

From the condition in the problem we have $\frac{2}{3}x = \frac{4}{5}y = \frac{8}{15}z$, so $5x = 6y = 4z$.

So $y = \frac{5}{6}x$ and $z = \frac{5}{4}x$.

Substitution in $x + y + z = 222$ gives:

$$x + \frac{5}{6}x + \frac{5}{4}x = 222$$

After solving this equation we obtain $x = 72$, $y = \frac{5}{6} \cdot 72 = 60$, $z = \frac{5}{4} \cdot 72 = 90$.
