## Supplied solution 1

Let $x$ be the number of days from the fourth day onward that Marko needs to finish the book. Then we have the equation
$$
22 + 2 \cdot (22 + 4) + x \cdot (22 + 4 + 2) = 242,
$$
from where we obtain $x = 6$. So Marko will read the book for $3 + x = 3 + 6 = 9$ days.
