## Supplied solution 1

Note that
$$
\frac{x^5 - x^2}{x^5 + y^2 + z^2} = 1 - \frac{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}{x^5 + y^2 + z^2}
$$
and its cyclic analogous forms. The given inequality is equivalent to
$$
\frac{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}{x^5 + y^2 + z^2} + \frac{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}{y^5 + z^2 + x^2} + \frac{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}{z^5 + x^2 + y^2} \le 3. \quad (\dagger)
$$
In view of the **Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality** and the condition $xyz \ge 1$, we have
$$
\begin{aligned} (x^5 + y^2 + z^2)(yz + y^2 + z^2) &\ge \left(x^{\frac{5}{2}}(yz)^{\frac{1}{2}} + y^2 + z^2\right)^2 \\ &\ge (x^2 + y^2 + z^2)^2, \end{aligned}
$$
or
$$
\frac{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}{x^5 + y^2 + z^2} \le \frac{yz + y^2 + z^2}{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}.
$$

Taking the cyclic sum of the above inequality and analogous forms gives
$$
\frac{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}{x^5 + y^2 + z^2} + \frac{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}{y^5 + z^2 + x^2} + \frac{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}{z^5 + x^2 + y^2} \le 2 + \frac{yz + zx + xy}{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}.
$$
It suffices to show that $xy + yz + zx \le x^2 + y^2 + z^2$, which is well known (and can be easily shown by the Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality or $(x - y)^2 + (y - z)^2 + (z - x)^2 \ge 0$).

## Supplied solution 2

Given a function $f$ of $n$ variables, we define the symmetric sum
$$
\sum_{\text{sym}} f(x_1, \dots, x_n) = \sum_{\sigma} f(x_{\sigma(1)}, \dots, x_{\sigma(n)})
$$
where $\sigma$ runs over all permutations of $1, \dots, n$ (for a total of $n!$ terms). For example, if $n = 3$, and we write $x, y, z$ for $x_1, x_2, x_3$,
$$
\begin{aligned} \sum_{\text{sym}} x^3 &= 2x^3 + 2y^3 + 2z^3 \\ \sum_{\text{sym}} x^2y &= x^2y + y^2z + z^2x + x^2z + y^2x + z^2y \\ \sum_{\text{sym}} xyz &= 6xyz. \end{aligned}
$$
If $xyz = t^3 \ge 1$, set $x = tx_1, y = ty_1$, and $z = tz_1$. Then $x_1, y_1$, and $z_1$ are real numbers with $x_1y_1z_1 = 1$. Note that
$$
\frac{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}{x^5 + y^2 + z^2} = \frac{x_1^2 + y_1^2 + z_1^2}{x_1^5 t^3 + y_1^2 + z_1^2} \le \frac{x_1^2 + y_1^2 + z_1^2}{x_1^5 + y_1^2 + z_1^2}
$$
We may further assume $xyz = 1$ for inequality (†). We establish inequality (†) in a very mechanical way. Multiplying both sides of the inequality by
$$
(x^5 + y^2 + z^2)(y^5 + z^2 + x^2)(z^5 + x^2 + y^2)
$$
and canceling the like terms reduces the desired inequality to
$$
\sum_{\text{sym}} (x^9 + x^3 + 4x^7y^5) \ge \sum_{\text{sym}} (x^6 + x^3y^3 + 2x^5y^4 + 2x^4y^2). \quad (\dagger')
$$
By the AM-GM Inequality, we have $x^6+y^6 \ge 2x^3y^3$ and $x^9+x^3 \ge 2x^6$ and their symmetric analogous forms. Adding them together shows that
$$
\sum_{\text{sym}} (x^9 + x^3) \ge \sum_{\text{sym}} (x^6 + x^3 y^3). \quad (*)
$$
It suffices to show that
$$
\sum_{\text{sym}} x^7 y^5 \ge \sum_{\text{sym}} x^5 y^4 = \sum_{\text{sym}} x^6 y^5 z \quad (**)
$$
and
$$
\sum_{\text{sym}} x^7 y^5 \ge \sum_{\text{sym}} x^4 y^2 = \sum_{\text{sym}} x^6 y^4 z^2. \quad (***)
$$

## Supplied solution 3

(Based on work by Jiayin Kang from China) We shall prove something more, namely that
$$
\frac{x^5}{x^5 + y^2 + z^2} + \frac{y^5}{y^5 + z^2 + x^2} + \frac{z^5}{z^5 + x^2 + y^2} \geq 1 \quad (\ddag)
$$
and
$$
1 \geq \frac{x^2}{x^5 + y^2 + z^2} + \frac{y^2}{y^5 + z^2 + x^2} + \frac{z^2}{z^5 + x^2 + y^2}. \quad (\ddag')
$$
Note that (‡) follows from adding
$$
\frac{x^5}{x^5 + y^2 + z^2} \geq \frac{x^4}{x^4 + y^4 + z^4} \quad (\text{or } x(y^4 + z^4) \geq y^2 + z^2)
$$
with its analogous cyclic inequalities. By the AM-GM Inequality, we have
$$
2x(y^4 + z^4) \geq x(y^2 + z^2)^2 \geq 2xyz(y^2 + z^2) \geq 2(y^2 + z^2),
$$
as desired.

## Supplied solution 4

(Based on work by Hyun Soo Kim) We present a third approach of establishing inequality (†). Because $y^2 + z^2 \geq 2yz$ and $xyz \geq 1$, we have
$$
\frac{1}{x^5 + y^2 + z^2} \leq \frac{1}{\frac{x^4}{yz} + y^2 + z^2} \leq \frac{1}{\frac{2x^4}{y^2+z^2} + y^2 + z^2}
$$
and the cyclic analogous forms. Thus it suffices to show that
$$
\frac{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}{\frac{2x^4}{y^2+z^2} + y^2 + z^2} + \frac{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}{\frac{2y^4}{z^2+x^2} + z^2 + x^2} + \frac{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}{\frac{2z^4}{x^2+y^2} + x^2 + y^2} \leq 3.
$$
However, since this is a homogeneous inequality, the condition $xyz \geq 1$ is not relevant anymore.
Furthermore, we may assume that $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 3$. Then the inequality reduces to
$$
\frac{1}{\frac{2x^4}{3-x^2} + 3 - x^2} + \frac{1}{\frac{2y^4}{3-y^2} + 3 - y^2} + \frac{1}{\frac{2z^4}{3-z^2} + 3 - z^2} \leq 1,
$$
or
$$
\frac{3 - x^2}{3x^4 - 6x^2 + 9} + \frac{3 - y^2}{3y^4 - 6y^2 + 9} + \frac{3 - z^2}{3z^4 - 6z^2 + 9} \leq 1,
$$
where $x, y$, and $z$ are positive real numbers with $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 3$.
Because $3x^4 - 6x^2 + 9 = 3(x^2 - 1)^2 + 6 \geq 6$ and $3 - x^2 = y^2 + z^2 \geq 0$, we obtain
$$
\frac{3 - x^2}{3x^4 - 6x^2 + 9} \leq \frac{3 - x^2}{6}.
$$
Adding the above inequality and the cyclic analogous forms gives
$$
\frac{3 - x^2}{3x^4 - 6x^2 + 9} + \frac{3 - y^2}{3y^4 - 6y^2 + 9} + \frac{3 - z^2}{3z^4 - 6z^2 + 9} \\ \leq \frac{9 - (x^2 + y^2 + z^2)}{6} = 1,
$$
as desired.

## Supplied solution 5

(Based on work by Xuancheng Shao from China) We claim that
$$
\frac{1}{x^5 + y^2 + z^2} \le \frac{\frac{3}{2} \cdot (y^2 + z^2)}{(x^2 + y^2 + z^2)^2}.
$$
Adding the above inequality and the cyclic analogous forms yields the desired inequality (†).
Because $xyz \ge 1$, $x \ge \frac{1}{yz}$, and so
$$
\frac{1}{x^5 + y^2 + z^2} \le \frac{1}{\frac{x^4}{yz} + y^2 + z^2},
$$
or
$$
\frac{1}{x^5 + y^2 + z^2} \le \frac{yz}{x^4 + yz(y^2 + z^2)}.
$$
It suffices to show that
$$
\frac{2yz}{x^4 + yz(y^2 + z^2)} \le \frac{3(y^2 + z^2)}{(x^2 + y^2 + z^2)^2},
$$
or
$$
2yz(x^2 + y^2 + z^2)^2 \le 3x^4(y^2 + z^2) + 3yz(y^2 + z^2)^2.
$$
Expanding the left-hand side of the last inequality in $x^2$ and $y^2 + z^2$ gives
$$
2x^4yz + 4x^2yz(y^2 + z^2) \le 3x^4(y^2 + z^2) + yz(y^2 + z^2)^2.
$$
Because $3x^4(y^2 + z^2) \ge 6x^4yz$, it suffices to show that
$$
4x^2yz(y^2 + z^2) \le 4x^4yz + yz(y^2 + z^2)^2,
$$
which is evident by the AM-GM Inequality.

## Supplied solution 6

(Based on work by Iurie Boreico from Moldova) Note that
$$
\frac{x^5 - x^2}{x^5 + y^2 + z^2} \ge \frac{x^5 - x^2}{x^3(x^2 + y^2 + z^2)}
$$
is equivalent to
$$
\frac{(x^3 - 1)^2(y^2 + z^2)}{x(x^5 + y^2 + z^2)(x^2 + y^2 + z^2)} \ge 0,
$$
which is true for all positive $x, y, z$. Hence
$$
\frac{x^5 - x^2}{x^5 + y^2 + z^2} \ge \frac{x^2 - \frac{1}{x}}{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}.
$$
Summing the above inequality with its analogous cyclic inequalities, we see that the desired result follows from
$$
x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - \frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{y} - \frac{1}{z} \ge 0.
$$
Since $xyz \ge 1$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
& x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - \frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{y} - \frac{1}{z} \\
= & x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - \frac{yz + xz + xy}{xyz} \\
\ge & x^2 + x^2 + z^2 - yz - xz - xy \\
= & \frac{(x-y)^2 + (y-z)^2 + (z-x)^2}{2} \ge 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
so we are done.
