## Supplied solution 1

Let Maja had $x$ denars with her. Then from the condition in the problem we have that
$$
\frac{65}{100}x + \frac{57.5}{100}x = 45 + x.
$$
So we obtain that
$$
65x + 57.5x = 4500 + 100x.
$$
$$
122.5x = 4500 + 100x
$$
$$
22.5x = 4500
$$
$$
x = 200.
$$
So Maja had $200$ denars with her.
