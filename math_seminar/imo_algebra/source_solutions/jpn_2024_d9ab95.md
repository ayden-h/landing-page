\boxed{\frac{122}{11}}
$$
We have
$$
\begin{align*}
\sqrt{\frac{123! - 122!}{122! - 121!}} &= \sqrt{\frac{123 \cdot 122! - 122!}{122 \cdot 121! - 121!}} = \sqrt{\frac{(123 - 1) \cdot 122!}{(122 - 1) \cdot 121!}} \\
&= \sqrt{\frac{122 \cdot 122 \cdot 121!}{121 \cdot 121!}} = \sqrt{\frac{122^2}{121}} = \frac{122}{11}.
\end{align*}
$$
