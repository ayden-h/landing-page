## Supplied solution 1

Let $x$ be the number of candies in the bag in the beginning. Then Ilina ate $\frac{1}{5}x + 3$ during the first day and there were $\frac{4}{5}x - 3$ candies left. During the second day Ilina ate $\frac{1}{5}(\frac{4}{5}x - 3) + 5$ and the third day she ate the remaining 15 candies. Hence
$$
\frac{1}{5}x + 3 + \frac{1}{5}(\frac{4}{5}x - 3) + 5 + 15 = x.
$$
Expanding:
$$
\frac{1}{5}x + 3 + \frac{4}{25}x - \frac{3}{5} + 5 + 15 = x
$$
Combine like terms:
$$
\left(\frac{1}{5}x + \frac{4}{25}x\right) + (3 - \frac{3}{5} + 5 + 15) = x
$$
$$
\frac{5}{25}x + \frac{4}{25}x = \frac{9}{25}x
$$
$$
3 - \frac{3}{5} + 5 + 15 = (3 + 5 + 15) - \frac{3}{5} = 23 - \frac{3}{5}
$$
So:
$$
\frac{9}{25}x + 23 - \frac{3}{5} = x
$$
Move all terms to one side:
$$
\frac{9}{25}x + 23 - \frac{3}{5} - x = 0
$$
$$
\frac{9}{25}x - x = -\frac{16}{25}x
$$
So:
$$
-\frac{16}{25}x + 23 - \frac{3}{5} = 0
$$
$$
-\frac{16}{25}x = \frac{3}{5} - 23
$$
$$
-\frac{16}{25}x = \frac{3 - 115}{5} = -\frac{112}{5}
$$
Multiply both sides by $-1$:
$$
\frac{16}{25}x = \frac{112}{5}
$$
Multiply both sides by $25$:
$$
16x = 25 \times \frac{112}{5} = 5 \times 112 = 560
$$
$$
16x = 560
$$
$$
x = 35
$$
So, there were $35$ candies in the bag in the beginning.
