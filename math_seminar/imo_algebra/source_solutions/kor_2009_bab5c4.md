## Supplied solution 1

Clearly
$$
B \ge \frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{c} + \frac{1}{a}
$$
and
$$
A - (a + b + c) = \frac{a^4 + b^4 + c^4 - a^2b^2 - b^2c^2 - c^2a^2}{(a+b)(b+c)(c+a)} \ge 0.
$$
Therefore we have
$$
AB \ge \left( \frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{c} + \frac{1}{a} \right) (b+c+a) \ge 9.
$$
by Cauchy-Schwarz inequality. This completes the proof. □
