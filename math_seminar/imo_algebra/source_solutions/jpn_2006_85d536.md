## Supplied solution 1

Let $n = 100A + 10B + C$. Then,
$$
n - m = (100A + 10B + C) - (A + 10B + 100C) = 99(A - C).
$$
Since $A$ and $C$ can be any natural number between $1$ and $9$, the maximum value of $n - m$ is $99 \cdot (9 - 1) = 792$.
