## Supplied solution 1

The numbers $ABC$ and $BAC$ are three-digit, so $A \neq 0$ and $B \neq 0$. If $C=1$ the equality $\overline{ABC} \cdot C = \overline{---A}$ is not possible because $\overline{AB} \cdot 1 = \overline{AB} \neq \overline{AB} \cdot 0$. If $A>3$, from the equality $\overline{ABC} \cdot A = \overline{---A}$ it follows that $\overline{ABC} \cdot A > 400 \cdot 4 = 1600 > 999$. Hence $A \leq 3$. If $A=1$, then $C=1$ which is impossible from the previous discussion. Therefore $A=2$ or $A=3$. If $A=3$ then from $\overline{ABC} \cdot A = \overline{---A}$ it follows that $C=1$ which is impossible. Hence $A=2$. Now, from $2\overline{BC} \cdot 2 = \overline{---2}$, $C=1$ or $C=6$. Therefore $C=6$. Finally from the equality $2\overline{B6} \cdot B = \overline{---B}$ it must $B \in \{1,2,4,6,8\}$. Checking all possibilities for $B$ gives that $B=8$.
