## Supplied solution 1

From the condition in the problem we get the equations
$$ \frac{2x+15}{8} - 1\frac{1}{3} \cdot (y-1) = \frac{2(5-2y)}{3} $$
and
$$ \frac{x+5}{2} = 3y + 0.125. $$
They are equivalent to
$$ 6x = 3 $$
and
$$ 4x - 24y = -22. $$
The solution to this system is
$$ x = \frac{1}{2} $$
and
$$ y = 1. $$
