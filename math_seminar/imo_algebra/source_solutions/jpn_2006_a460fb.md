## Supplied solution 1

By adding the three equations and simplifying, we get
$$
(x - 1)^2 + (y - 2)^2 + (z - 3)^2 = 0.
$$
So it is necessary that $(x, y, z) = (1, 2, 3)$. And this satisfies the given equations.
