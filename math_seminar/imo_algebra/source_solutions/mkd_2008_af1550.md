## Supplied solution 1

In the first barrel there is $5$ hl $25$ l $= 525$ l of wine. So in the second barrel there is $3 \cdot 525$ l $= 1575$ l of wine and in the third $5$ hl $25$ l $- 1$ hl $75$ l $= 525$ l $- 175$ l $= 350$ l of wine. In the three barrels there is $525$ l $+ 1575$ l $+ 350$ l $= 2450$ l $= 24$ hl $50$ l of wine in total.
