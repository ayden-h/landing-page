## Supplied solution 1

Let $a$, $b$ and $c$ be distinct positive integers with sum of any two of them being squares. We may assume that $a < b < c$. Write $a + b = x^2$, $b + c = y^2$, $c + a = z^2$. Then we shall minimize $x^2 + y^2 + z^2$ under the conditions $x < y < z$, $z^2 < x^2 + y^2$, and $x^2 + y^2 + z^2$ even. $z > 5$, since if otherwise $z^2 \ge x^2 + y^2$.
If $z = 6$, $x$ and $y$ must be both even or both odd, but neither is appropriate since $6^2 > 5^2 + 3^2 > 4^2 + 2^2$.
If $z = 7$, only $(x, y, z) = (5, 6, 7)$ satisfies the required conditions.
If $z \ge 8$, $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 > 2z^2 \ge 2 \times 8^2 > 7^2 + 6^2 + 5^2$.
Therefore $(x, y, z) = (5, 6, 7)$ satisfies the required conditions and minimizes $x^2 + y^2 + z^2$. So the answer is $(a, b, c) = (6, 19, 30)$.
