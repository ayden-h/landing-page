## Supplied solution 1

Dividing both sides by $a$, $b$, $c$, the inequality is equivalent to
$$
\frac{a(b-c)}{c(a+b)} + \frac{b(c-a)}{a(b+c)} + \frac{c(a-b)}{b(c+a)} \geq 0.
$$
By adding $1+1+1$ to both sides, the inequality becomes
$$
\frac{b(c+a)}{c(a+b)} + \frac{c(a+b)}{a(b+c)} + \frac{a(b+c)}{b(c+a)} \geq 3.
$$
By applying AM-GM inequality we have
$$
\frac{b(c+a)}{c(a+b)} + \frac{c(a+b)}{a(b+c)} + \frac{a(b+c)}{b(c+a)} \geq 3\sqrt{\frac{b(c+a) \cdot c(a+b) \cdot a(b+c)}{c(a+b) \cdot a(b+c) \cdot b(c+a)}} = 3.
$$
The inequality is proved.
