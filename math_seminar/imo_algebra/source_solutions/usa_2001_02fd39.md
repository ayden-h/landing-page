## Supplied solution 2

Let $D$ and $Q$ be the feet of the perpendiculars from $B$ and $P$ to line $AC$, respectively. Then $DQ \leq BP$. Furthermore, the given conditions imply that $AP^2 > BP^2 + PC^2$, which can be written as $AP^2 - PC^2 > BP^2$. Hence,
$$
\begin{align*}
AQ^2 &\geq AQ^2 - QC^2 \\
&= (AP^2 - PQ^2) - (CP^2 - PQ^2) \\
&= AP^2 - PC^2 \\
&> BP^2 \\
&\geq DQ^2.
\end{align*}
$$
Let $\ell$ be the ray $AC$ minus the point $A$. Note that, since $PA > PC$, $Q$ lies on ray $\ell$. If $D$ did not lie on $\ell$, then $AQ$ would be less than or equal to $DQ$, a contradiction. Thus, $D$ lies on $\ell$, and angle $BAC$ is acute.

## Supplied solution 4

We first prove the following Lemma.

**Lemma.** For any four points $W$, $X$, $Y$, and $Z$ in the plane,
$$
WY^2 + XZ^2 \leq WX^2 + XY^2 + YZ^2 + ZW^2.
$$
*Proof.* Pick an arbitrary origin $O$ and let $w$, $x$, $y$, $z$ denote the vectors from $O$ to $W$, $X$, $Y$, $Z$, respectively. Then
$$
\begin{align*}
WX^2 + XY^2 + YZ^2 + ZW^2 - WY^2 - XZ^2 \\
&= |w-x|^2 + |x-y|^2 + |y-z|^2 + |z-w|^2 - |w-y|^2 - |x-z|^2 \\
&= w \cdot w + x \cdot x + y \cdot y + z \cdot z \\
&\quad -2(w \cdot x + x \cdot y + y \cdot z + z \cdot w - w \cdot y - x \cdot z) \\
&= |w+y-x-z|^2,
\end{align*}
$$
which is always nonnegative. Equality holds if and only if $w + y = x + z$, which is true if and only if $WXYZ$ is a (possibly degenerate) parallelogram.

Applying the Lemma to points $A$, $B$, $C$, and $P$ gives
$$
\begin{align*}
0 \le AB^2 + BP^2 + PC^2 + CA^2 - AP^2 - BC^2 \\
&= (PB^2 + PC^2 - PA^2) + (AB^2 + AC^2 - BC^2) \\
&< 0 + (AB^2 + AC^2 - BC^2) \\
&= AB^2 + AC^2 - BC^2.
\end{align*}
$$
Therefore, angle $BAC$ is acute.

## Supplied solution 5

In this solution, $\sin^{-1}$ takes on values between $0^\circ$ and $90^\circ$. Note that $\angle PAB < 90^\circ$, since $PB < PA$. Applying the **Law of Sines** to triangle $PAB$ yields
$$
\sin \angle PAB = \frac{PB}{PA} \sin \angle ABP \le \frac{PB}{PA}.
$$
It follows that
$$
\angle PAB \le \sin^{-1} \frac{PB}{PA}.
$$
Since $PA^2 > PB^2 + PC^2$, we have similarly
$$
\angle PAC \le \sin^{-1} \frac{PC}{PA} < \sin^{-1} \frac{\sqrt{PA^2 - PB^2}}{PA}.
$$
Thus,
$$
\begin{align*}
\angle BAC &\le \angle BAP + \angle PAC \\
&< \sin^{-1} \frac{PB}{PA} + \sin^{-1} \frac{\sqrt{PA^2 - PB^2}}{PA}.
\end{align*}
$$
If $\theta = \sin^{-1} \frac{PB}{PA}$, then $\sin(90^\circ - \theta) = \cos \theta = \sqrt{1 - \sin^2 \theta} = \frac{\sqrt{PA^2 - PB^2}}{PA}$.
Hence,
$$
\angle BAC < \sin^{-1} \frac{PB}{PA} + \sin^{-1} \frac{\sqrt{PA^2 - PB^2}}{PA} = 90^\circ,
$$
and angle $BAC$ is acute.
