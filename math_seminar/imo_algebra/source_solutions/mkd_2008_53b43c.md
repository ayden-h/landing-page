## Supplied solution 1

If we denote the side of the square by $a$, then the side lengths of the rectangle are $2a$ and $a+22$. The sum of the two side lengths of the rectangle with length $2a$ is equal to the perimeter of the square. Hence the sum of the other two side lengths, with length $a+22$, is $2000\text{ mm}$. Now we obtain $2a+44=2000$, $2a=1956$, $a=1956:2=978\text{ mm}$.
