## Supplied solution 1

From the determining formula for $(a_n)$ it is easily to find that
$$
a_n = (2^n + 3^n)^{\frac{1}{n}} \quad \forall n \ge 1.
$$
For all $n \ge 1$, we have:
$$
\begin{align*}
2^n + 3^n > 3^n &\Rightarrow (2^n + 3^n)^{n+1} > 3^n (2^n + 3^n)^n > (2^{n+1} + 3^{n+1})^n \\
&\Rightarrow (2^n + 3^n)^{\frac{1}{n}} > (2^{n+1} + 3^{n+1})^{\frac{1}{n+1}}.
\end{align*}
$$
Thus $a_n > a_{n+1} \quad \forall n \ge 1$, or $(a_n)$ is decreasing.
