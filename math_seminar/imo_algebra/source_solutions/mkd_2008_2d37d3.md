## Supplied solution 1

The area of the resulting square is $P_{sq} = (a + b)^2 = 100\,\mathrm{cm}^2$, hence $a + b = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$. Because $a$ and $b$ are positive integers we have the following cases: $a = 1$, $b = 9$; $a = 2$, $b = 8$; $a = 3$, $b = 7$; $a = 4$, $b = 6$; $a = 5$, $b = 5$; $a = 6$, $b = 4$; $a = 7$, $b = 3$; $a = 8$, $b = 2$; and $a = 9$, $b = 1$. The rectangle with smallest area is obtained for $a = 1$, $b = 9$ or $a = 9$, $b = 1$. Its area is $9\,\mathrm{cm}^2$.
