## Problem

Let $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$ be distinct positive integers, $n \geq 3$. Prove that there exist distinct indices $i$ and $j$ such that $a_{i}+a_{j}$ does not divide any of the numbers $3 a_{1}, 3 a_{2}, \ldots, 3 a_{n}$.
