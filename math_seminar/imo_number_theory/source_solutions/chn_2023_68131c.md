## Supplied solution 1

Let $n = d + r$ and $m = d \cdot (d+r) + 1$. Then, for $1 \le b \le d+r$, the number of integers in the range $1, 2, \dots, m$ that are coprime to $b$ is given by
$$
\frac{\varphi(b)}{b} \cdot d \cdot (d+r)! + 1.
$$
Note that $b$ divides $(d+r)!$, so this number is congruent to $1$ modulo $b$. Therefore,
$$
|S(m, n)| \equiv \sum_{b=1}^{d+r} 1 \equiv r \pmod{d}.
$$
